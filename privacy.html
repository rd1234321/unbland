Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Unbland is a tool that builds a website from a description of a business. This page explains what information we handle when you use it, in plain language.

Who we are

Unbland is run by Riley Durbin, based in California, United States. You can reach us at unblandapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you type. The business descriptions you enter and the change requests you send in the chat.
  • The sites you make. If you sign in, we store your sites so you can come back to them, including any photos or logos you upload into them.
  • Your account details. If you create an account, your email address and a securely stored password, or, if you use Google sign-in, your email address and the basic profile details Google shares.
  • Usage records. Each time a site is built or changed by the AI we record the time, the style used, and how much AI processing it took. To enforce daily limits we store a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet address. We do not store the address itself.

We do not use advertising trackers. The site keeps you signed in using storage in your own browser.

How we use it

  • To build, save, edit and publish your sites.
  • To keep the service within its limits and prevent abuse.
  • To understand how the service is used and improve it.
  • To contact you about your account if we need to.

The design library

When a site turns out well, we may keep its design (the layout, colors, fonts and animation) so it can be reused for other businesses. Before a design is kept, the business name, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, place names and people's names are removed, and the design is checked to confirm they are gone. Sites containing photos or logos you uploaded are never added to the library.

Who else handles your information

We use other companies to run the service. They receive only what they need to do their part:

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved sites and uploaded images, and runs our server code.
  • Anthropic provides the AI. The descriptions you type, your chat requests, and the content of the site being worked on are sent to Anthropic to be processed.
  • Pixabay provides stock photos. We send it short search phrases describing the kind of business, such as "dental office". We do not send it your name, your account, or the business's name.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • GitHub hosts this website and the pages that display published sites.

Sites made with Unbland load their fonts from Google Fonts, so a visitor's browser contacts Google when it opens one. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Published sites are public

When you press Publish, your site is available to anyone who has its link, and it may be found by others. Only publish information you are happy to make public. You can take a published site offline at any time.

Who at Unbland can see your sites

The people who run Unbland can view saved sites in order to operate the service, fix problems, and choose designs for the design library.

How long we keep things

We keep your saved sites and account until you delete them or ask us to. Usage records are kept for as long as they are useful for running the service. If you are not signed in, your site is held only in your own browser and is lost when you close the tab.

Your choices

  • You can delete any saved site from "My sites". Deleting it also takes it offline if it was published.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything stored with it by emailing unblandapp@gmail.com.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at the same address.

"Do Not Track" signals

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Unbland does not track you across other websites and does not use advertising trackers, so it behaves the same way whether or not that signal is sent.

Children

Unbland is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
