What goes into a taco

The tortilla

Corn tortillas, handmade and pressed to order. Masa goes flat, hits the comal and puffs. That is why it folds without cracking.

The meat

Al pastor comes off the trompo as orders come in. Asada is grilled and chopped. Birria is cooked down until it pulls apart.

The finish

Tacos are topped with salsa and onion. Vegetarians have rajas con queso, which is built the same way as the rest.

Before you order

How many tacos is a meal?

Most people order three. Tacos are small, so it is easy to start with two and come back to the window.

What is consommé?

It is the rich broth the birria cooks in. You dip the taco into it, or sip it between bites.

What can a vegetarian eat?

The rajas con queso taco is vegetarian. Ask at the window if you have any other questions about ingredients.

The truck

The truck pulls up at your event and serves from the window, the same way it does behind the brewery.

It suits a crowd that likes to wander over, order and watch the tortillas hit the comal. It needs a spot where a truck can park.

The taco bar

No truck at the door. Fillings and tortillas are set out and guests build their own plates.

It suits a banquet room, an office kitchen or a backyard where a truck will not fit, and it lets everyone eat at the same time.

Which events it fits

  • PartiesBirthdays, graduations and backyard get-togethers. Tacos are easy to eat standing up, and guests can pick their own filling.
  • WeddingsA late-night taco window is a familiar way to feed guests after the dinner, or a relaxed main meal for the whole day.
  • Office lunchesA taco bar keeps a team lunch quick. Rajas con queso covers the vegetarians in the room.

Questions people ask

How many guests can you serve?

Catering runs from 25 guests up to 200.

Are there vegetarian options?

Yes. Rajas con queso is the vegetarian taco.

How do I get a quote?

Call and describe the event. Pricing depends on the setup and the guest count, so it is worked out in that conversation.

What should I think about before booking?

Know your date, your headcount and where the food will be served. For the truck, check that it can park close to your guests, and whether the venue allows outside food vendors.
