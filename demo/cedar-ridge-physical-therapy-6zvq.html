Four kinds of care, one hour each visit.

Here is what each service involves, how a session runs and how payment works. Every visit is with a doctor of physical therapy.

01

Sports injuries

Sprains, strains and overuse problems usually come from load that outran the body's ability to handle it. Treatment works on the injured area and on what led to it.

  • Sprains and strains
  • Overuse problems such as sore tendons
  • Getting back to training in stages
02

Back and neck pain

Pain in the back or neck often changes how you sit, sleep and move. Physical therapy looks at posture, strength and movement habits to find what keeps it going.

  • Stiffness and recurring pain
  • Trouble sitting, sleeping or lifting
  • Exercises you can keep doing at home
03

Post-surgery rehab

After an operation, strength and range of motion need to come back in a sensible order. Rehab rebuilds them step by step, working with what your surgeon has advised.

  • Regaining range of motion
  • Rebuilding strength
  • Returning to daily activity
04

Running gait analysis

A gait analysis is a close look at how you run. It helps show where strain builds up, so changes can target the cause and not only the sore spot.

  • Runners with repeated injuries
  • Pain that shows up at a certain distance
  • Anyone curious how they move

Common questions about physical therapy

What is a doctor of physical therapy?

A DPT is a clinician trained to assess how you move, find what is limiting you and treat it with exercise and hands-on care.

Do I need to be in pain to come in?

No. People also come after surgery, to return to a sport, or because something feels off and keeps getting in the way.

What should I wear?

Clothes you can move in, and shoes you would exercise in. If you are being seen for a knee, hip or shoulder, pick something that lets the area be reached.

Will it hurt?

Some effort and mild soreness are normal in rehab. Sharp or worsening pain is worth telling your therapist about right away.
