Our story

Two siblings and one hearth

Daniel and Marisol Reyes opened Ember & Oak in 2021, inside a 1920s brick warehouse on Lexington Avenue.

Daniel cooked for eight years in wood-fired kitchens in Oaxaca and San Francisco. Marisol runs the dining room and the natural wine list.

The cooking leans on nearby producers: vegetables from Laurel Branch Farm, trout from Cold Spring Hollow, beef from Ridgeback Cattle Co. and flour from Old Mill Grain.

The hearth

How a wood fire cooks

The hearth is lit at 3pm with white oak and hickory. Oak burns steady and clean, while hickory adds a deeper smoke.

As the evening moves on, the fire changes. Open flame chars and sears, glowing coals roast slowly and evenly, and warm ash gently cooks root vegetables right in their skins.

The sourdough goes in last, once the fire has settled into an even heat.

Questions

Questions

A few things people often ask about wood-fired cooking and dinner here.

What does cooking over live fire change about the food?

Direct flame caramelizes surfaces quickly and adds light smoke, while coals give a steady radiant heat for slower roasting. The result is char on the outside and tenderness inside, with flavor from the wood itself.

Why does the menu change every week?

The menu follows what local farms and producers have at their best. Changing it weekly lets the kitchen cook with the season rather than around it.

Can I come without a reservation?

The eight-seat cocktail bar welcomes walk-ins. For a table in the dining room, reserve online or call (828) 555-0162.

What is a natural wine?

Natural wines are generally made with minimal intervention, from farmed grapes fermented with native yeasts and little or no additives. They can taste livelier and less uniform than conventional wines.

The room

Your own table at the hearth

The private dining room seats up to 20 guests inside our 1920s brick warehouse on Lexington Avenue. It suits birthdays, rehearsal dinners, team dinners and any evening that calls for a long table.

Your meal comes from the same wood-fired kitchen as the dining room, cooked over live flame, hot coals and warm ash with local white oak and hickory.

Marisol runs the dining room and the natural wine list, and can help you choose bottles to suit your menu.

Booking questions

A few quick answers before you book.

Do I need a reservation?

For a table in the dining room, yes, we recommend reserving online or by phone. The eight-seat cocktail bar takes walk-ins.

What if I have an allergy?

Add it to the notes on the form or mention it when you call. The menu changes weekly, so details are best shared before you arrive.

Can I book the chef's tasting?

The five-course chef's tasting is $85, with a wine pairing for $45. Mention it in your notes and see the menu for details.

Can I host a group of more than 8?

Yes. The private dining room seats up to 20 guests, with set menus from $75 a person. See Private Dining.
