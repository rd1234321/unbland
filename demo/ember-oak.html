One fire, three temperatures.

Local white oak and hickory burn from 3pm. As the wood breaks down, the hearth sorts itself into zones, and every dish finds the heat it needs. The sourdough goes in last, as the fire settles.

Live flame

Fast, bright heat that blisters and chars. Smoke clings to the edges.

Hot coals

Steady, even radiant heat for longer cooking and deep browning.

Warm ash

Low, gentle heat that roasts slowly and softens from the inside.

What the room offers.

The room sits inside the 1920s brick warehouse, apart from the main floor but still facing the fire. Your guests hear the evening without being part of it.

It suits a long, shared table where one conversation can run from the first plate to the last.

How a set menu works.

A set menu means everyone at the table eats the same courses, served together. It keeps the evening moving and lets the kitchen cook for the whole room at once. Set menus start at $75 a person, and the details are worked out with you.
