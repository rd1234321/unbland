Lena Ortiz, baker

Rise & Crumb is Lena's bakery. She bakes in small batches, which means a short list of things made carefully, every morning, rather than a long list made ahead.

Small-batch baking runs on timing. Sourdough needs a long, slow fermentation, often started the day before. Croissant dough is folded around cold butter again and again to build its layers. None of it can be rushed, so the day starts long before the doors open.

When something sells out, it is gone until the next bake. Coming early is the best way to get first pick.

Questions

A few things people often ask about fresh bread and pastry.

06 / 07
01How should I store a sourdough loaf?

Keep it cut side down on a board or in a paper or cloth bag for the first day or two. Plastic softens the crust. For longer, slice it and freeze it, then toast slices straight from the freezer.

02Why does sourdough taste different from regular bread?

Sourdough rises with a natural starter of wild yeast and bacteria instead of packaged yeast. The long fermentation gives a gentle tang, a chewier crumb and a crust that stays crisp longer.

03How do I refresh a croissant the next day?

A few minutes in a moderate oven brings back the crisp outer layers. Skip the microwave, which turns laminated pastry soft and chewy.

04How far ahead should I ask about a custom cake?

Custom cakes are made to order, so the sooner the better, especially around holidays and busy weekends. The custom cakes page explains how to send a request.

The menu

On the counter

Four things baked fresh each morning, and what goes into each one.

Sourdough

Sourdough loaves

Naturally leavened loaves with a crisp, blistered crust and a chewy crumb. They rise on a natural starter, not packaged yeast.

The dough ferments slowly, often starting the day before the bake. That long rise is where the gentle tang and the crust come from.

  • Best on day oneKeep it cut side down on a board or in a paper bag.
  • Keeps for longerSlice it and freeze it, then toast slices from frozen.
Croissants

Croissants

Laminated by hand. Cold butter is folded into the dough again and again, which builds the thin layers that bake up flaky and crisp.

Lamination cannot be hurried, because the butter has to stay cold and the dough has to rest between folds.

  • Next dayA few minutes in a moderate oven brings back the crisp layers.
  • Skip the microwaveIt turns laminated pastry soft and chewy.
Cinnamon buns

Cinnamon buns

Soft dough rolled by hand around a spiced cinnamon filling, then baked in the pan so the buns rise snug against each other.

They are best warm from the morning bake. They are popular, so come early for the best chance at one.

Galettes

Fruit galettes

A galette is a free-form tart: a flat round of pastry, fruit in the middle, and the edges folded up over it. No pan, no lattice, just buttery crust and fruit.

The fruit is whatever is in season, so the galette on the counter changes through the year.

How it changes

The core bakes stay. The fruit follows the calendar.

Custom cakes

Cakes made to order

Tell Lena what you are celebrating and she will bake a cake for it at Rise & Crumb in Asheville.

01  Ordering

How it works

Custom cakes are made to order, not pulled from the counter. The process is simple and starts with a request.

1

Send a request

Fill in the form below with your date, the size you need and your ideas.

2

Talk it through

We get back to you to go over flavors, design and anything that needs working out.

3

Confirm the details

Once the plan is settled, your cake goes on the baking schedule for your date.

4

Pick it up

Collect your cake at 18 Haywood Road, Wednesday to Sunday between 7am and 2pm.

02  Before you ask

What to decide

You do not need every answer to get started. Having a rough idea of these makes the first conversation quicker.

The date

When you need the cake. Busy weekends and holidays fill up, so earlier is better.

The size

How many people will be eating. Count guests, then think about whether you want leftovers.

The flavor

Cake, filling and frosting are all choices. Tell us what you like and what you avoid.

The look

Simple and rustic, or decorated for an occasion. A photo or a few words about the mood helps.

Allergies

Mention any allergies or dietary needs up front so they can be talked through properly.

Pickup

Pickup is at the bakery during opening hours. Pick a day and time that fits your event.

03  Request

Ask for a cake

Send the details you have. A request is not a booking, it starts the conversation.

Prefer to talk in person? Stop by 18 Haywood Road, Wednesday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm.

04  Good to know

About custom cakes

01How early should I ask?

As early as you can. Each cake is made to order, so popular dates fill up first.

02Do I need a finished design?

No. A date, a rough guest count and a sense of what you like is enough to begin.

03How should I store the cake?

Keep it somewhere cool and out of direct sun. Cakes with buttercream or fresh fillings hold best in the refrigerator, then sit out for a while before serving so the flavor and texture soften.
